# TOP Product Documentation

## What TOP is

TOP is a Web2 platform for people who want to move something real forward.

It helps a person turn an observation, question, offer, or idea into a clear next action; bring the right people together when help is needed; and leave a trace of what was actually made, learned, or improved.

TOP is not built to keep people scrolling. It is built to help them return to real life with more clarity, more capability, and more momentum.

> Don't just be online. Move something real.

Its practical loop is:

```text
Signal → Seed → Project → Action → Evidence → Reflection → Growth
```

Every part of TOP exists to strengthen that loop.

## The problem TOP is trying to solve

Most social platforms reward attention: posting often, reacting quickly, watching longer, and appearing popular. That produces a lot of activity, but not always a lot of progress.

TOP takes a different position:

- An idea should be able to become a real commitment.
- A commitment should have a next action, not just a title.
- Collaboration should be intentional and small enough to stay human.
- Reputation should come from useful work, care, and follow-through, not follower counts.
- Technology should protect attention instead of extracting it.

The goal is not to make people feel important online. The goal is to help them become more capable, connected, creative, and useful offline.

## The TOP promise

TOP gives people two connected spaces:

1. A public shared field to discover people, ideas, questions, offers, and useful resources.
2. A private Field where a person can think, make, plan, collaborate, and keep their real work moving.

The public space creates possibility. The private space protects focus.

## The core building blocks

| Building block | Meaning in TOP | What it becomes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Signal | A public idea, need, offer, question, resource, negotiation, event, or collaboration call. | A useful conversation or an origin for deeper work. |
| Seed | A private early-stage commitment worth tending. | A project when it has enough clarity and intention. |
| Project | A focused piece of work with purpose, a next action, milestones, and evidence. | A completed outcome and a legacy. |
| Evidence | A note, decision, Atelier piece, Studio canvas, Blueprint, link, contribution, or review that shows movement. | A credible trail of real work. |
| Circle | The small group around a project. | A human collaboration space, not a crowd. |
| Reflection | A short review of what moved, what was learned, and what matters next. | Better judgment and more deliberate growth. |

## The public TOP Page

The TOP Page is the platform's shared public field. It is not a traditional engagement feed.

Members can share a signal as an:

- Idea
- Useful information
- Offer
- Question
- Negotiation
- Need
- Resource
- Moment of movement
- Gathering
- Collaboration call

Each signal asks for a meaningful response. TOP's reactions are deliberately named after intent:

| Response | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Spark | “This opened something for me.” |
| Build | “I would help make this real.” |
| Help | “I can contribute.” |
| Ask | “I have a useful question.” |
| Respect | “This is thoughtful work.” |

People can also add public responses, see who moved a signal, visit a member's public profile, or offer help, a skill, or collaboration. The goal is to make intention visible, not to manufacture empty popularity.

### Discovery

TOP Page includes search for:

- People by name, field, location, or biography
- Public signals by title, content, or type

Private Fields, private project details, private messages, and private collaboration material never appear in public search.

## Signal → Seed → Project

This bridge is one of TOP's most important ideas. A public signal does not have to remain a passing post.

### 1. A person shares or finds a signal

For example: “I want to start a robotics club for beginners in my neighborhood.”

### 2. The signal can become a private seed

The author, or another member, can bring that signal into their own private Field. TOP creates a seed with the original signal as its source, then the person can clarify the problem, desired outcome, and next action privately.

### 3. The author can open a project circle

When the signal is ready for action, its author can create a project. They choose:

- A direction: personal, creative, learning, community, venture, or other
- One concrete next action
- One first milestone

TOP creates a protected project in their Field and connects it back to the originating signal.

### 4. Interested people can offer something useful

Other members can offer help, a skill, or collaboration. The project owner decides whether to welcome them into the circle. Nothing becomes a commitment without a clear decision.

## The private Field

The Field is a member's protected workspace. It is where private work happens and where projects have room to develop without becoming a public performance.

It includes:

- The member's private projects and seeds
- Project progress, milestones, activity, and reflections
- Collaboration invitations and notifications
- A project command center for each commitment
- Making and planning tools

No other TOP member can browse someone else's Field. A public profile can show selected public identity and public work, but never private workspace content.

## The Project Command Center

Every project is meant to have a real home, not just a card with a percentage.

Its command center brings together:

- Mission: why the project exists
- Outcome: what it is trying to make possible
- Next action: the smallest meaningful move to make now
- Milestones: visible progress from planned to completed
- Evidence trail: work created in tools, notes, links, contributions, and reviews
- Activity timeline: a record of movement in the project
- Circle: collaborators, invitations, roles, and conversation
- Weekly reflection: what moved forward, what was learned, and what comes next

When a project is complete, its history becomes a legacy rather than disappearing into a timeline.

## Making tools inside the Field

TOP includes native tools that help a person turn intention into visible work.

### Atelier

Atelier is the beginning space: choose a direction, make a first version, and record a meaningful piece of work in a project evidence trail.

### Studio

Studio is a touch- and mouse-friendly drawing canvas. A member can sketch, change colours, undo, export a PNG, keep a local canvas preview, and link the work to a project as evidence.

### Blueprint

Blueprint is a visual path mapper. A member can connect things to learn, make, test, and share; rearrange the map; and attach the resulting path to a project.

These tools are not meant to replace professional design, writing, or development software. They give every TOP member a friction-light place to begin, then let them connect the result to meaningful work.

## Profiles, connection, and messages

A TOP profile has two layers.

### Public profile

Other signed-in members can see only intentionally public information, including:

- Name and profile picture
- Field name, location, and member-since date
- Public project and evidence counts
- Open public commitments
- Recent visible movement
- Public signals, their reactions, and responses

From here, a person can send a connection invitation.

### Private profile dashboard

The owner sees their own overview, identity, Field, Signals, Messages, and account settings. This dashboard is the navigation center for their TOP life.

### Connections and direct messages

Connections are mutual. A person sends an invitation, the recipient accepts or declines it in Signals, and only accepted connections can use direct messages. This avoids an open, noisy inbox and keeps conversations intentional.

Project invitations, offers, and public responses appear in Signals. Direct conversations belong in Messages, not mixed into general notifications.

## Trust, privacy, and boundaries

TOP needs clear lines between what is shared and what is protected.

| Area | Visibility |
| --- | --- |
| Public signals and public responses | Visible to signed-in TOP members |
| Public profile details and shared work | Visible to signed-in TOP members |
| Field, seeds, project details, evidence, and reflections | Private to the owner and accepted project members where relevant |
| Project-circle messages | Visible only to people in that project circle |
| Direct messages | Visible only to the two accepted connections |

TOP should never sell the illusion that public visibility equals growth. A person can build meaningful things quietly.

## How TOP measures progress

TOP is intentionally not organized around likes, watch time, or follower growth. Useful signals still receive responses, but the deeper measures are things such as:

- Skills practiced or learned
- Projects begun and finished
- Milestones honoured
- Evidence recorded
- People helped or mentored
- Circles joined or strengthened
- Ideas moved into real action
- Reflections kept

The principle is simple: digital activity should strengthen a person's real life, not replace it.

## Design principles

Every product decision should pass these tests:

1. Does this help someone move something real forward?
2. Does this protect attention rather than exploit it?
3. Does it reward contribution, craft, care, or learning?
4. Does it leave room for beginners as well as experts?
5. Does it make the private work of becoming easier, not more performative?

This is why TOP favours projects over posts, circles over crowds, evidence over vanity metrics, and reflection over endless reaction.

## The Three.js world

The Three.js experience is a visual language for TOP, not a virtual world people should escape into.

It gives spatial form to real activity: fields, seeds, routes, projects, connections, and movement. It should always be grounded in actual work coming from the platform and should never become a substitute for life outside the screen.

TOP does not require Blender, Web3, wallets, tokens, NFTs, or blockchain. It is deliberately a modern Web2 platform built with conventional accounts, permissions, APIs, and a relational database.

## What exists in TOP today

The current product includes:

- Account registration, sign-in, sign-out, and profile editing
- Public TOP Page with public signals, responses, reactions, people, and work discovery
- Public profiles and connection invitations
- Private Fields with seeds and projects
- Project command centers with milestones, evidence, activity, reflections, collaborators, invitations, and circle messages
- Direct messages for accepted connections
- Signals and notifications for invitations, responses, offers, and reactions
- Atelier, Studio, and Blueprint tools connected to project evidence
- Responsive Vue interface and Three.js-powered workspace experience

## What TOP can grow into

TOP's long-term direction can include:

- Richer project legacy pages for completed work
- Better mentorship and apprenticeship paths based on recognised craft
- More powerful project-circle planning and collaboration
- A more visual map of knowledge, contribution, and inspiration
- Carefully designed growth summaries that help members reflect without turning life into a score
- Community tools for schools, neighbourhoods, creative groups, and mission-driven teams

New features should be added only when they strengthen the core loop. If something creates more passive consumption than real movement, it does not belong in TOP.

## The TOP manifesto

We believe every person can learn.

We believe people become stronger by making, practising, helping, and reflecting.

We believe reputation should be earned through actions.

We believe knowledge becomes more valuable when it is shared.

We believe communities become stronger when people contribute.

We believe technology should amplify human creativity, not replace it.

We believe attention is precious and should never be exploited.

We believe success is not how long a person stays online, but how much they grow because they were here.

TOP is not an escape from reality. It is a bridge for improving it.
